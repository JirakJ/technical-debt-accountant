# Technical Debt Accountant — JetBrains Marketplace Listing

## Plugin Name
Technical Debt Accountant

## Tagline
Quantify technical debt in dollars — make refactoring a business decision.

## Short Description
Track technical debt with real dollar costs, ROI calculators, and management-ready reports. Turn "we need to refactor" into a data-driven business case your stakeholders understand.

## Full Description

<p>
Every developer knows technical debt exists. The problem is <em>proving it</em> to stakeholders. "We need to refactor" doesn't get budget. "$4,200/month in workaround costs with a 3-month payback period" does. <strong>Technical Debt Accountant</strong> bridges the gap between developer intuition and business language by quantifying technical debt in dollars, tracking workaround costs, and generating management-ready reports — all from within your IDE.
</p>

<h3>Key Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Debt Inventory</strong> — Catalog debt items across 9 categories (design, code, testing, documentation, infrastructure, dependency, performance, security, architecture) with 4 priority levels.</li>
  <li><strong>Quick Add</strong> — Press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> to register a debt item right where you spot it, linked to the exact file and line.</li>
  <li><strong>Workaround Timer</strong> — Track time spent on workarounds with manual entry or a built-in timer. Every minute is recorded and converted to cost.</li>
  <li><strong>Hotspot Detection</strong> — Identifies files with the highest concentration of debt items so you know where to focus refactoring efforts.</li>
  <li><strong>Git Blame Integration</strong> — Track when debt was introduced and by whom. See debt age and origin at a glance.</li>
  <li><strong>Gutter Icons</strong> — Visual markers in the editor gutter with debt tooltips — see debt context without leaving your code.</li>
  <li><strong>Git-Tracked Storage</strong> — Debt data lives in <code>.tech-debt/inventory.json</code>, committed to your repo so the whole team shares one source of truth.</li>
  <li><strong>Dollar Valuation</strong> — Convert workaround time to dollars using configurable hourly rates. See the real cost of living with debt. <em>(Pro)</em></li>
  <li><strong>Monthly Interest Calculation</strong> — Calculate the recurring monthly cost of each debt item based on accumulated workaround data. <em>(Pro)</em></li>
  <li><strong>ROI Calculator</strong> — Estimate fix cost, compare to ongoing workaround expense, and calculate breakeven period. Data-driven refactoring prioritization. <em>(Pro)</em></li>
  <li><strong>Burndown Charts</strong> — Visualize debt reduction over time. Track progress and celebrate wins. <em>(Pro)</em></li>
  <li><strong>Management Reports</strong> — Export polished Markdown reports with dollar figures, ROI analysis, and prioritized recommendations — ready for stakeholder meetings. <em>(Pro)</em></li>
  <li><strong>Team Aggregation</strong> — Aggregate debt data across developers and projects for organization-wide visibility. <em>(Enterprise)</em></li>
  <li><strong>JIRA Integration</strong> — Link debt items to JIRA tickets for seamless workflow integration. <em>(Enterprise)</em></li>
  <li><strong>Long-Term Trend Analytics</strong> — Track debt trends over months and quarters to measure improvement. <em>(Enterprise)</em></li>
</ul>

<h3>Getting Started</h3>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Install</strong> — Search "Technical Debt Accountant" in <em>Settings → Plugins → Marketplace</em> and click Install.</li>
  <li><strong>Log Your First Debt</strong> — Spot a workaround or code smell? Press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>D</kbd> to catalog it with category, priority, and description.</li>
  <li><strong>Track &amp; Report</strong> — Use the workaround timer to record time spent, then open the <em>Tech Debt</em> tool window to view hotspots, costs, and generate reports.</li>
</ol>
